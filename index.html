Install the app
Put Marble Fighter on your home screen: it opens full screen and plays offline.
Layout
Touch gestures, or ◀ ▶ FIRE buttons at the bottom.
Move withbuttons layout
◀ ▶ buttons, or a slider: drag along it and the robot slides to the matching spot on its rail.
Fire buttonsbuttons layout
One FIRE button, or three that fire front-left, front and front-right.
Left / Right
Buttons and A / D slide the unit or turn its aim.
Mouse aim
Cursor aims, click fires, right-click uses an item.
Shooting line
Haptics
A tap under your thumb for every shot, and harder ones for hits, parries and knockouts. Android, and iPhone on iOS 18 or later.
Music
A soundtrack for the menus, fights, bosses and Gate Rush that builds as a fight heats up. Sound effects stay on either way (the speaker on the home screen mutes everything).
Music credits
Free tracks from OpenGameArt. “Jungle Jumpin’” by Scott Elliott (Scrabbit), loop edit by AntumDeluge, CC-BY 4.0. Public domain (CC0): “Treasure Hunter” by TAD; “Space Battle”, “Space City”, “Hard Dungeon” and “Heavy Dungeon” by MintoDog; “Fields of Ice” by Jonathan So; “Bamboo Blitz” by Tsorthan Grove; “Desert Overworld” by KarateStudios; “Determined Pursuit” by Emma_MA.
Orientation
Portrait keeps the game upright. Landscape plays it on its side (phones that allow it turn for you; otherwise turn yours). Auto follows the way you hold the device.
Camera
From a high chase view down to the cockpit.
Realismgraphics
Marble hops, spin and shadows, stage particles and full screen resolution.
Cel shadinggraphics
Banded toon lighting with ink outlines. Off for the smooth, glossy look.
Battery savergraphics
Runs cooler and lasts longer on a phone: a little less resolution and glow, lighter shadows and 30 frames a second.
Special effectsgraphics
Overlay rings, glows, trails, flashes, sparks, smoke and afterimages.
Health display
Top bar like a fighting game, or a health tag that floats over each fighter and follows it.
FPS meter
Shows frames per second in the corner, to check performance.
Pack tacticswith a pack
Who they gang up on: the nearest of your side, the weakest (to knock robots out fast), or always you.

How well your CPU pack-mates aim, lead, dodge and react.

Extra rules for Duel. Boss Ladder always plays a clean table.

Unlock allfor trying them out
Fighters normally unlock as you clear arcade stages, and their rewards as they level up. This opens every one now; your arcade record and levels still count.
Your packyou + CPU pack-mates
Bring up to two allies; they share your rail in their own lanes. Bigger packs get less armour and damage each, like the opponent's.
Packopponents at once
Fight up to three. Bigger packs get less armour and damage each.
Pucks
Pushable cover for trick shots. Applies next match.
Wallscover per side
Crates in front of each rail that block shots. Break after 5 hits.
Pinspinball posts
Bouncy posts that redirect balls. Placed so both sides get the same angles.
Magazinemarbles per load
Per fighter: each fighter's magazine comes with its marble (light marbles carry more, heavy ones fewer). Equal: both carry the size below.
Fire rateballs per press
How many marbles leave the barrel on each trigger pull, in a quick string. Applies to both.
Power gates
Sliding gates mid-table: 5× multiplies a ball's power, POW makes a power ball, DRG splits it into a dragon shot, HOM turns it into a homing steel ball, WALL blocks until shot 5 times.
Charge shots
Opponents hold fire to charge a big shot now and then: watch for the glow at their muzzle, and dodge or shield.
