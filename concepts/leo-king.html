Leo King: five royal directions

Concept sheets for redesigning Leo King to read as a king at a glance. Each keeps its role: fist cannons that fire alternately, the "royal" special pose, chin up and leaning back. Pick one, or mix parts, and it goes to 3D.

Today: red lion head, gold shoulders, fist cannons and a cape, but no crown or royal regalia, so it reads as a warrior more than a king.
