A tap under your thumb for every shot, and harder ones for hits, parries and knockouts. Android, and iPhone on iOS 18 or later.
Music

Camera

Extra rules for versus. Arcade always plays a clean table.

Unlock all fightersfor trying them out
Fighters normally unlock as you clear arcade stages. This opens every one now; your arcade record still counts.
Your packyou + CPU pack-mates
Bring up to two allies; they share your rail in their own lanes. Bigger packs get less armour and damage each, like the opponent's.
Packopponents at once
Fight up to three. Bigger packs get less armour and damage each.
Pucks
Pushable cover for trick shots. Applies next match.
Wallscover per side
Crates in front of each rail that block shots. Break after 5 hits.
Pinspinball posts
Bouncy posts that redirect balls. Placed so both sides get the same angles.
Magazinemarbles per load
Per fighter: each fighter's magazine comes with its marble (light marbles carry more, heavy ones fewer). Equal: both carry the size below.
Fire rateballs per press
How many marbles leave the barrel on each trigger pull, in a quick string. Applies to both.
Power gates
Sliding gates mid-table: 5× multiplies a ball's power, POW makes a power ball, DRG splits it into a dragon shot, HOM turns it into a homing steel ball, WALL blocks until shot 5 times.
Charge shots
Opponents hold fire to charge a big shot now and then: watch for the glow at their muzzle, and dodge or shield.
